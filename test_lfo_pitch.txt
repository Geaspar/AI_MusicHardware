Test Steps for LFO Pitch Modulation:

1. Run ./bin/AIMusicHardwareIntegrated
2. In the Modulation Routing section:
   - Row 1: Set Source = "LFO 1", Destination = "Pitch", Amount = 50% (or drag slider)
3. Play a note on the keyboard (click on a key)
4. You should hear the pitch wobbling up and down
5. Adjust the LFO 1 Rate slider to change the speed of modulation
6. Adjust the modulation amount slider to change the depth

Expected behavior:
- When LFO is connected to Pitch, the note's pitch should oscillate
- The console should show "Pitch modulation LFO value" messages
- The pitch should vary by the amount set (50% = 6 semitones)
