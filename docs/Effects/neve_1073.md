## Neve 1073‑Style Preamp/EQ — Class‑A Color & Musical EQ (Aug 2025)

A preamp/EQ inspired by the classic Neve 1073: transformer‑coupled Class‑A color with a stepped 3‑band EQ and high‑pass filter. Focus on vibe (iron, saturation, impedance interaction) and the signature musical curves/frequencies. Adds modern conveniences (mix, trim, OS) while preserving character. Vital reference: filters/saturation building blocks and per‑block smoothing patterns.

### Goals
- Authentic color: transformer saturation and gentle THD (2nd/3rd) with level‑dependent tone
- Musical EQ curves: stepped frequencies and broad Q reminiscent of 1073
- HPF steps with classic corner options
- RT‑safe, CPU‑aware; oversampling for saturation path

---

## Feature Overview
- Preamp (Color section)
  - Input Trim and Output Trim
  - Transformer model (low‑order nonlinearity + frequency‑dependent tilt)
  - Class‑A style soft clip (waveshaper) with level‑calibrated gain staging
  - Impedance interaction emulation (subtle LF/HF contour varying with input level)
  - Oversampling: Off/2×/4× (auto at high drive)

- EQ Section (order: Preamp → HPF → Low Shelf → Mid Bell → High Shelf)
  - High Shelf: fixed 12 kHz ±16 dB
  - Mid Bell (peaking): stepped freq: 360 Hz, 700 Hz, 1.6 kHz, 3.2 kHz, 4.8 kHz, 7.2 kHz; gain ±18 dB; Q broad (fixed)
  - Low Shelf: stepped freq: 35, 60, 110, 220 Hz; gain ±16 dB
  - High‑Pass Filter (HPF): stepped: 50, 80, 160, 300 Hz (12 dB/oct)
  - EQ In/Out switch; Preamp only; EQ only modes

Enhancements (optional, default OFF)
- Pre/EQ order switch (for creative routing)
- Mid Bell Q variation (Narrow/Broad) while maintaining 1073 feel
- Mic/Line toggle (changes internal headroom/transformer saturation threshold)
- Mix (parallel) for EQ (for creative blending)

## Architecture
- Preamp color path
  - Pre‑emphasis (very gentle tilt) into transformer nonlinearity
  - Transformer model: low‑order even/odd harmonic gen with frequency‑dependent coefficient (LF head‑bump subtle; HF roll gently with drive)
  - Soft clipper: tanh/asin hybrid with calibrated makeup to preserve perceived loudness; oversampling if Drive/Color high

- EQ path (RBJ biquads)
  - HPF: 12 dB/oct stepped cutoff
  - Low Shelf: RBJ shelf at stepped freqs; curves tuned to broad musical contour
  - Mid Bell: peaking filter with fixed/broad Q; stepped frequency list
  - High Shelf: fixed 12 kHz RBJ shelf

- Routing & gain staging
  - Default: Preamp → HPF → Low Shelf → Mid → High Shelf → Mix → Output Trim
  - Internal level normalizer between Preamp and EQ to stabilize EQ behavior under drive

Safeguards & polish
- Parameter smoothing (10–30 ms) for gains and Color; stepped switches crossfade over ~10 ms
- Denormal guards; no allocations in `process`
- Oversampling only around nonlinear block; latency negligible/hidden
- Constant‑power Mix for parallel EQ or color blending
